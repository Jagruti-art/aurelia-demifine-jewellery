The Demi-Fine Revolution

What Exactly is Demifine® Jewellery?

Traditional fine gold jewellery costs a fortune and stays locked inside bank lockers out of fear. Fashion jewellery tarnishes in weeks and turns your skin green with cheap brass and nickel alloys.

Aurelia Demifine® bridges the gap: genuine 925 sterling silver and medical-grade surgical steel, sealed in 2.5 microns of real 18K gold vermeil. You get the opulent weight and shine of real gold at a price that lets you collect every style guilt-free.
